# Netflix Data Visualization using Matplotlib

## Project Overview

This project explores the Netflix Movies and TV Shows dataset through data visualization using Matplotlib. The objective is to transform raw data into meaningful visual representations that uncover trends, patterns, and insights about Netflix's content library.

---

## Objectives

- Transform raw data into visual formats
- Create informative charts using Matplotlib
- Identify trends and patterns
- Build a compelling data story
- Support decision-making through visualization

---

## Dataset

- **Dataset:** Netflix Movies and TV Shows
- **Source:** Kaggle
- **File:** `netflix_titles.csv`

---

## Tools and Libraries

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib

---

## Visualizations Created

1. Distribution of Movies and TV Shows
2. Content Released by Year
3. Top 10 Countries Producing Netflix Content
4. Top 10 Content Ratings
5. Top 10 Netflix Genres
6. Distribution of Movie Durations
7. Monthly Content Additions
8. Top 10 Directors

---

## Key Insights

- Movies dominate Netflix's catalog.
- Netflix experienced rapid content growth between 2015 and 2019.
- The United States contributes the largest amount of content.
- TV-MA is the most common content rating.
- International Movies and Dramas are the most popular genres.
- Most Netflix movies are between 80 and 120 minutes long.
- July records the highest number of content additions.
- Several directors have contributed multiple titles to the platform.

---

## Conclusion

The project demonstrates how data visualization can transform raw datasets into meaningful insights. By using Matplotlib, various aspects of Netflix's content library were explored, helping to identify trends, audience preferences, and content distribution patterns.
